ABOUT COGNI-ROUTINE

The thinking behind the product

A calm, chat-first routine designed to make everyday wellbeing check-ins easier to understand and easier to continue.

◌

Personal baseline

The product looks at a person’s own repeated routine rather than comparing them with a generic score or another person.

+

Small daily signals

Conversation, language, memory, spatial, symbol, and response activities create a gentle picture of engagement over time.

↗

Persistent change matters

One unusual day is not treated as a conclusion. The system looks for repeated patterns and presents them as descriptive information.

i

Safety first

Cogni-Routine is wellbeing tracking and a mild cognitive activity, not medical advice, diagnosis, screening, or a replacement for professional care.

↻

One guided daily loop

The server keeps the routine in order: conversation, five short activities, and completion. The web client renders the current step rather than deciding what comes next.

भ

Built for real conversations

English, Hindi, and Marathi support makes the routine more familiar, while voice input remains optional and is treated as a capture signal, not a speech diagnosis.

▦

Five complementary domains

Language, memory, spatial recall, symbol matching, and response timing are observed across repeated sessions instead of reduced to one isolated number.

≈

Quality before interpretation

Sleep, illness, device changes, typing context, and incomplete days are kept as data-quality context so ordinary variation is not over-interpreted.

□

Research stage

This is a prototype for prospective validation with Indian-language users. Future evaluation should compare adherence and patterns with an accepted clinical reference under human oversight.
